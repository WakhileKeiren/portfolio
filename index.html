About
Design is infrastructure for human connection.

Wakhile Keiren Mavimbela is a 22-year-old creative technologist and digital health advocate based in Eswatini. Founder of Keiren Creative Studios, a freelance design and digital content practice.

His work spans brand identity, social media campaigns, UI/UX design, and digital health tools. He built PhiliGo — a gamified TB adherence app for children — and TuckHub, a campus food ordering app. He has served as Technical Director for the Mental Health Society at UNESWA, designing all social media content and campaigns.

He writes about identity, purpose, and the systems that shape us. He believes good design isn't decoration — it's how we make care visible, accessible, and human.
